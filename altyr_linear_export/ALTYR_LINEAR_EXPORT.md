Link to OF implementation: [https://onlyfans.com/my/release-forms](https://onlyfans.com/my/release-forms)

OnlyFans primarily detects the need for a model release form through **user tagging** and a combination of **automated content moderation systems** and **human review**

If unverified individuals appear in content, the system flags it for review and requires the creator to provide documentation.

**OnlyFans Detection Process**

* **Verified Collaborator Tagging:** The primary method is a built-in system where creators tag the verified accounts of other models in their content. This tag automatically confirms the collaborator's age (18+) and consent, negating the need for a separate form.
* **Automated Content Scanning:** OnlyFans employs "state-of-the-art digital technologies" (including AI and computer vision) to pre-screen all uploaded content for restricted material and unverified faces.
* **Human Moderation & Flagging:** Content that passes the initial AI scan is manually reviewed by trained human moderators. These moderators identify and escalate any content featuring individuals who are not verified or lack a signed release form.
* **Reactive Measures:** If unverified content is posted, OnlyFans flags it and typically gives the creator 48 hours to submit the proper documentation. Failure to comply results in content removal or account suspension.
