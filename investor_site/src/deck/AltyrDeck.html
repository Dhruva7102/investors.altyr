The problem

Agencies running like real businesses, on tools built for one person.

A modern agency runs a chatter team across dozens of creator accounts. OnlyFans' native dashboard has no concept of an agency, a team, or a roster. So operators stitch together logins, spreadsheets, and guesswork. That's not a competitor to build against; it's an absence to fill.
