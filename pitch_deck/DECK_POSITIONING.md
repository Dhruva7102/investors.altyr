# Altyr Pitch Deck — Positioning Spec (v2)

**Last updated:** 2026-06 · Drives all slide content in `src/components/slides/`.

## One-liner
Altyr is the **operating system for OnlyFans agencies (Altyr Pro)**, plus a **premium
monetization platform for their highest-value fans (the Altyr platform)**. One company,
two products, one industry.

## The two products
**Altyr Pro — B2B SaaS (the wedge)**
- CRM + chatter-management infrastructure for OF agencies, built on OFAPI.
- Connects every creator into one place; live chatter-performance visibility;
  standardized relationship playbooks; fan tiers (new sub → whale); AI that audits
  and coaches chatters.
- Pricing: **$250 / connected creator / month.**
- Status: final testing with agency contacts; ~1 month from stable at scale.
- Its own sub-LLC under Altyr.

**Altyr platform — premium fan destination (the upside)**
- Premium destination for high-value fans ("whales").
- Model: **20% commission on whale GMV** (creator keeps 80%); Altyr bears processing.
- Launches AFTER Pro — Pro builds the relationship + data, platform monetizes whales.

## Go-to-market
- Agency-led land-and-expand. Sign agencies → they bring entire rosters + fanbases.
- **No paid acquisition.** Pro first, platform second.
- Target: top ~15–20 OF agencies, each managing 150+ creators.

## Unit economics
- Altyr Pro: $250 / connected creator / month (recurring SaaS).
- Altyr platform: 20% of whale GMV.
- Per-creator: ~$13K gross rev/creator/mo (roster avg) × 60% whale share ×
  whale→Altyr penetration × 20% commission, plus $250 SaaS.
- Revenue mix at scale: ~83% platform commission, ~17% SaaS.
